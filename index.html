01 · UX Internship · 2026

QuantivRisk

A UX design internship at an InsurTech company that uses vehicle data to determine fault in crashes. I designed CRASHVIEW’s reanalysis flow, audited and redesigned the company website and wrote the platform’s user guide.

Problem
Users had no safe way to fix accident details or videos after a crash was analyzed.
What I did
Designed the reanalysis flow and prototype, audited and redesigned the website, and wrote the user guide.
Outcome
Edits create Analysis 2 while Analysis 1 is kept, and users see exactly what changed.

02 · Mobile app · 2026

Adulting

With one partner, I interviewed 13 young adults and designed a mobile assistant that pulls their tasks, habits and daily briefings into one place. I led the prototype in Figma.

Problem
Young adults living alone for the first time juggle bills, errands and deadlines with no real system — and feel overwhelmed.
What I did
Co-led 13 interviews and the synthesis, mapped 6 user flows and designed the Figma prototype.
Outcome
A clickable prototype covering 6 core flows, grounded in 13 user interviews.

03 · E-commerce · 2026

Second Hand Blues

I designed and built a full-stack e-commerce site for my own vintage clothing brand, directing Claude as a design partner while making every design call myself.

Problem
Selling vintage on Instagram and Depop meant stale inventory, endless scrolling and no way to ask for a specific style.
What I did
Designed and built everything (brand, UX, UI and front end), directing Claude as a design partner.
Outcome
A launched, working e-commerce site for a real business.
